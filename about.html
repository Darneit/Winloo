Company Introduction

From Kerala to the Kingdom.

Winloo Contracting Company LLP was founded in 2017 in Kerala, India, with its headquarters in Inchakkal, Trivandrum. The company profile records expansion into Saudi Arabia in 2023.

Its contracting scope includes civil and infrastructure works, steel fabrication and erection, industrial piping, PEB installations, maintenance and related project execution. The live website adds architectural, interior, lighting and BIM services.
