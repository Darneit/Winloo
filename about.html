About Winloo

Built on precision.
Growing across regions.

Winloo Contracting Company LLP is an industrial contracting and construction company with operations in India and Saudi Arabia.

Company Overview

From Kerala to the Kingdom.

Founded in 2017 in Kerala, India, and headquartered in Inchakkal, Trivandrum, Winloo grew through civil, infrastructure, steel and industrial work before establishing its Saudi Arabia branch in 2023.

A 500+ skilled workforce and modern machinery support projects of varying scale and technical complexity.

Workforce & Resources

People, plant and fabrication capability.

The resource base includes cranes, welding machines, hydraulic lifts, concrete mixers and heavy-duty transport vehicles, supported by CNC and cutting machinery in fabrication workshops.

Quality / Safety / Sustainability

Built into project execution.

Projects undergo quality checks at each phase, safety is embedded through occupational health and safety practices, and sustainability efforts include responsible sourcing, waste management and energy-efficient design.

Winloo describes its quality and safety systems as ISO-compliant, alongside local and international project standards.

India + Saudi Arabia

Two-country operating presence.

India remains Winloo’s base of origin and headquarters location. Saudi Arabia is the company’s key international operating market, with its branch established in 2023 and office in Al Khobar.
